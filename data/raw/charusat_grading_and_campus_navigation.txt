# CHAROTAR UNIVERSITY OF SCIENCE AND TECHNOLOGY (CHARUSAT)
# ACADEMIC GRADING SYSTEM, CGPA/SGPA RULES & 120-ACRE CAMPUS MAP DIRECTORY

================================================================================
1. CHARUSAT 10-POINT ACADEMIC GRADING SYSTEM & SGPA/CGPA CALCULATION
================================================================================
• Grading Scale:
  - Grade O (Outstanding): 90% to 100% | Grade Point = 10.0
  - Grade A+ (Excellent): 80% to 89% | Grade Point = 9.0
  - Grade A (Very Good): 70% to 79% | Grade Point = 8.0
  - Grade B+ (Good): 60% to 69% | Grade Point = 7.0
  - Grade B (Above Average): 50% to 59% | Grade Point = 6.0
  - Grade C (Average): 45% to 49% | Grade Point = 5.0
  - Grade P (Pass): 40% to 44% | Grade Point = 4.0
  - Grade F (Fail): Below 40% | Grade Point = 0.0 (Backlog / Remedial required)

• Formula for SGPA (Semester Grade Point Average):
  SGPA = Sum(Course_Credits * Grade_Points) / Sum(Course_Credits)

• Formula for CGPA (Cumulative Grade Point Average):
  CGPA = Sum(All_Semester_Credits * All_Semester_SGPA) / Total_Credits_Attempted

• Percentage Equivalent Formula:
  Percentage (%) = (CGPA - 0.5) * 10 or CGPA * 9.5 (As per university transcript guidelines).
