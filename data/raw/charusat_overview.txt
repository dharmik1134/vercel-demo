# CHAROTAR UNIVERSITY OF SCIENCE AND TECHNOLOGY (CHARUSAT)

## About University
CHARUSAT is a premier university located in Changa, Anand district, Gujarat, India. It was accredited with 'A+' Grade by NAAC. The campus is spread across 120+ acres of lush green environment with state-of-the-art infrastructure.

## Institutes & Constituent Colleges
1. **CSPIT** - Chandubhai S. Patel Institute of Technology (Computer Engineering, IT, AI & ML, Civil, Mechanical, EC, EE)
2. **DEPSTAR** - Devang Patel Institute of Advance Technology and Research (CSE, IT)
3. **CMPICA** - Chandaben Mohanbhai Patel Institute of Computer Applications (MCA, BCA, M.Sc IT, B.Sc IT)
4. **RPCP** - Ramanbhai Patel College of Pharmacy (B.Pharm, M.Pharm, Ph.D.)
5. **PDPIAS** - P. D. Patel Institute of Applied Sciences (Chemical, Physical, Biological Sciences)
6. **I2IM** - Indukaka Ipcowala Institute of Management (MBA, BBA)
7. **MTIN** - Manikaka Topawala Institute of Nursing (B.Sc Nursing, GNM, M.Sc Nursing)
8. **ARIP** - Ashok & Rita Patel Institute of Physiotherapy (BPT, MPT)
9. **CIPS** - Charusat Institute of Paramedical Sciences

## Admissions & Eligibility
- **B.Tech Programs**: Admission is governed by ACPC (Admission Committee for Professional Courses), Gujarat and through JEE Main / GUJCET scores. Management quota seats are filled based on merit.
- **BCA / MCA / B.Sc / M.Sc**: Direct merit-based admission on university portal (charusat.ac.in).
- **Scholarships**: CHARUSAT provides merit-cum-means scholarships, late Shri Chhotabhai B Patel scholarship, and sports/cultural fee concessions.

## Campus Facilities & Hostels
- Central Library with over 100,000+ volumes, IEEE, Springer, and ScienceDirect access.
- On-campus AC and Non-AC Hostels for Girls and Boys with high-speed Wi-Fi, mess, and 24/7 security.
- High-Performance Computing (HPC) Supercomputing Facility (Param Shavak / AI GPU labs).
- Sports Complex with synthetic tennis courts, cricket ground, gymnasium, and indoor sports arena.

## Placement & Training
- CHARUSAT Training and Placement Cell (T&P) connects students with top recruiters including TCS, Infosys, Adani, L&T, Evosys, Crest Data Systems, Byju's, and Amazon.
- Highest package recorded: INR 32+ LPA.
- Strong support for student entrepreneurship and incubation via CHARUSAT Startup and Innovation Policy (SSIP).
