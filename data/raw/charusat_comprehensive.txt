3. ADMISSIONS, ELIGIBILITY & SCHOLARSHIPS
================================================================================

3.1 Admission Routes & Criteria
• B.Tech Admissions (CSPIT & DEPSTAR):
  - 50% Seats allocated through ACPC Gujarat (Admission Committee for Professional Courses) via GUJCET merit rank.
  - 50% All India Management / NRI / Vacant seats allocated through JEE Main / GUJCET + 12th PCM merit percentage on CHARUSAT portal.
• BCA, B.Sc IT, BBA, B.Pharm, B.Sc, BPT, B.Sc Nursing: Direct merit admission on university online application portal.
• MCA, MBA, M.Tech, M.Pharm: Governed by ACPC Gujarat (through CMAT / PGCET / GPAT / GATE) and University NRI/Management quota.

3.2 University Scholarships & Financial Aid
• Merit Scholarship: Up to 100% tuition fee waiver for top board/GUJCET/JEE percentile rankers.
• Late Shri Chhotabhai B. Patel Scholarship: Financial support for economically backward meritorious students.
• Govt. Scholarships: MYSY (Mukhyamantri Yuva Swavalamban Yojana), Post-Matric SC/ST/OBC scholarships, and Pragati/Saksham AICTE schemes facilitated by the university scholarship desk.
• Sports Concession: Up to 50% waiver for state/national level athletes and tournament winners.
