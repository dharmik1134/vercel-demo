# Official Knowledge Repository for CHARUSAT Virtual Intelligence

=== UNIVERSITY IDENTITY, ACCREDITATIONS & GLOBAL RECOGNITION ===
• Full Name: Charotar University of Science and Technology (CHARUSAT)
• Location: CHARUSAT Campus, Off Nadiad-Petlad Highway, Changa - 388421, District Anand, Gujarat, India.
• Establishment: Established under Gujarat Act No. 8 of 2009 by Shri Charotar Moti Sattavis Patidar Kelavani Mandal (est. 1994).
• NAAC Accreditation: Accredited with "A+" Grade by the National Assessment and Accreditation Council (NAAC). First private university in Gujarat to receive "A" Grade in its very first cycle (2016).
• Government Recognition: Recognized as a "Center of Excellence" by the Government of Gujarat.
• Global Membership: Member of the United Nations Academic Impact (UNAI).
• National Institutional Ranking Framework (NIRF): Consistently ranked in the 151–200 Band in the "Universities" category in India; Ramanbhai Patel College of Pharmacy (RPCP) ranked Top 100 (#98 in NIRF) nationally.

=== 9 CONSTITUENT INSTITUTES & ACADEMIC FACULTIES ===
1. Chandubhai S. Patel Institute of Technology (CSPIT):
   - Established: 2000 (First institute on campus).
   - Degrees Offered: B.Tech, M.Tech, Ph.D.
   - Departments & Intake:
     * Computer Engineering (CE): 180 seats
     * Information Technology (IT): 120 seats
     * Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning (AI & ML): 60 seats
     * Electronics & Communication Engineering (EC): 60 seats
     * Electrical Engineering (EE): 60 seats
     * Mechanical Engineering (ME): 60 seats
     * Civil Engineering (CL): 60 seats
   - Accreditation: Approved by AICTE, New Delhi; NBA Accredited departments.

2. Devang Patel Institute of Advance Technology and Research (DEPSTAR):
   - Established: 2017 (Next-gen computing institute).
   - Departments & Intake:
     * Computer Science and Engineering (CSE): 300 seats
     * Information Technology (IT): 180 seats
     * Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning (AI & ML): 120 seats
   - Features: Advanced AI, Big Data Analytics, Cloud Computing, Cyber Security, and NVIDIA GPU Labs.

3. Smt. Chandaben Mohanbhai Patel Institute of Computer Applications (CMPICA):
   - Programs: BCA, B.Sc (IT), MCA, M.Sc (IT), Dual Degree BCA+MCA, Ph.D.
   - Highlights: State-of-the-art Apple iOS Lab, Full-Stack Web Development, Data Science, and near 100% placement rate.

4. Ramanbhai Patel College of Pharmacy (RPCP):
   - Programs: B.Pharm, M.Pharm (Pharmaceutics, Pharmacology, Quality Assurance, Clinical Pharmacy), Ph.D.
   - Recognition: Approved by Pharmacy Council of India (PCI) and AICTE; NIRF Top 100 ranked.
   - Facilities: Industrial pharmacy pilot plant, central analytical instrumentation laboratory (HPLC, GC, UV-Vis, FTIR).

5. Indukaka Iswarbhai Patel Institute of Management (I2IM):
   - Programs: MBA (Dual Specialization in Finance, Marketing, HR, Business Analytics, International Business), BBA, Dual Degree BBA+MBA, Ph.D.
   - Focus: Entrepreneurship, live corporate internships, case study pedagogy, stock market simulation lab.

6. P. D. Patel Institute of Applied Sciences (PDPIAS):
   - Departments: Biological Sciences (Biotechnology, Microbiology, Biochemistry), Physical Sciences (Physics), Chemical Sciences (Chemistry), Mathematical Sciences (Applied Mathematics).
   - Programs: B.Sc, M.Sc, Ph.D. with heavy focus on sponsored research and published papers in high-impact international journals.

7. Manikaka Topawala Institute of Nursing (MTIN):
   - Programs: B.Sc Nursing, M.Sc Nursing, Post Basic B.Sc Nursing, GNM.
   - Recognition: Approved by Indian Nursing Council (INC) and Gujarat Nursing Council (GNC). Clinical training directly at CHARUSAT Multispecialty Hospital.

8. Ashok & Rita Patel Institute of Physiotherapy (ARIP):
   - Programs: Bachelor of Physiotherapy (BPT), Master of Physiotherapy (MPT in Musculoskeletal, Neurological, Cardiopulmonary, Sports, Pediatrics), Ph.D.
   - Training: On-campus OPD, rehabilitation units, sports therapy clinics.

9. Charotar Institute of Paramedical Sciences (CIPS):
   - Programs: B.Sc in Medical Laboratory Technology (MLT), Medical Radiology & Imaging Technology, Operation Theatre & Anaesthesia Technology, Optometry.

=== SPACE RESEARCH, SUPERCOMPUTING & HIGH-TECH LABS ===
• Charusat Space Research and Technology Center (CSRTC):
  - Established in 2013 in collaboration with Space Applications Centre (SAC), ISRO, Ahmedabad (MoU signed Feb 3, 2014).
  - Flagship Project: Design and fabrication of the "SARASWATI" Nanosatellite under the ISRO Student Satellite Program.
  - Joint ISRO funded projects and Junior Research Fellowships (JRFs).
• Dr. K. C. Patel Research and Development Centre (KRADLE):
  - Dedicated university-level R&D center for multidisciplinary materials science, renewable energy, and nanotech research.
• High-Performance Computing (HPC) & Supercomputing:
  - PARAM Shavak Supercomputer set up in collaboration with GUJCOST and C-DAC.
  - Deep Learning and GPU Cluster powered by NVIDIA Tesla V100 Tensor Core GPUs.
  - NVIDIA Deep Learning Institute (DLI) certified curriculum and training.
• Charusat Startup and Innovation Centre (CSIC) & EDIC:
  - University incubation center supporting student innovation, Student Startup and Innovation Policy (SSIP), prototype funding, patent filing support, and startup venture mentoring.
