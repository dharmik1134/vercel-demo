3. TROUBLESHOOTING: "WI-FI CONNECTED BUT NO INTERNET / LOGIN PAGE NOT OPENING"
================================================================================
• Step 1: Force Captive Portal Trigger:
  Open Chrome/Safari/Edge and navigate to http://neverssl.com or http://172.16.0.1:8090 (do NOT use https).
• Step 2: Disable Private/Randomized MAC address or Custom DNS (e.g. 8.8.8.8) in phone Wi-Fi settings so that the campus DHCP server assigns the proper 172.16.x.x IP.
• Step 3: Keep the login status tab open in background to maintain active session.
