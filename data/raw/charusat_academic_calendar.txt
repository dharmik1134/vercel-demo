# CHAROTAR UNIVERSITY OF SCIENCE AND TECHNOLOGY (CHARUSAT)
# OFFICIAL ACADEMIC CALENDAR, EXAMINATION SCHEDULE & HOLIDAY LIST

=== ACADEMIC YEAR CALENDAR (SEMESTER TERMS & TIMELINES) ===

1. ODD SEMESTER SCHEDULE (Semesters 1, 3, 5, 7):
• Commencement of Classes (Odd Sem): First Week of July
• Mid-Semester Internal Examination - 1: Mid September
• Mid-Semester Internal Examination - 2 / Practical Exams: Late October
• End of Teaching (Odd Sem): Mid November
• University End Semester Regular Theory Examinations: Late November to Early December
• Semester Break / Winter Vacation for Students: Late November to Mid December
• Remedial & Supplementary Examinations (Odd Sem): Early to Mid December

2. EVEN SEMESTER SCHEDULE (Semesters 2, 4, 6, 8):
• Commencement of Classes (Even Sem): Mid December (approx Dec 16)
• Annual University Convocation: January / February
• Annual Sports & Cultural Mega Fest (SPOURAL & VRUND): Mid February
• Mid-Semester Internal Examination - 1: Mid February
• Mid-Semester Internal Examination - 2 / Practical Submissions: Late March
• End of Teaching (Even Sem): Mid April
• University End Semester Regular Theory Examinations: Late April to Mid May
• Summer Vacation & Summer Industry Internship Break: Mid May to Late June
• Remedial & Backlog Examinations (Even Sem): Early June

=== OFFICIAL PUBLIC & UNIVERSITY HOLIDAY CALENDAR ===

The university campus, administrative offices, and constituent institutes (CSPIT, DEPSTAR, CMPICA, RPCP, I2IM, PDPIAS, MTIN, ARIP, CIPS) observe the following official holidays:

1. JANUARY:
• January 14: Makar Sankranti (Uttarayan) — Official Holiday
• January 15: Vasi Uttarayan — Official Holiday / University Closed
• January 26: Republic Day (National Holiday & Flag Hoisting on Campus) — Holiday after ceremony

2. FEBRUARY:
• Maha Shivratri (Mid February) — Official Holiday
• Mid February: SPOURAL (University Sports Festival) & Cultural Days (Special Academic Event)

3. MARCH:
• March 4 (or Phalguna Purnima): Dhuleti (Holi Color Festival) — Official Holiday
• March 21: Ramzan Eid (Eid-ul-Fitr) — Official Holiday
• March 26: Shree Ram Navami — Official Holiday

4. APRIL:
• Mahavir Jayanti (Early April) — Official Holiday
• April 14: Dr. B. R. Ambedkar Jayanti — Official Holiday
• Good Friday (Mid April) — Official Holiday

5. MAY / JUNE:
• May 1: Gujarat Day (Gujarat Sthapana Divas) — Observance
• Bakrid / Eid-ul-Adha (Mid June) — Official Holiday
• Summer Vacation for Students: Mid May to Late June

6. AUGUST:
• August 15: Independence Day (National Holiday & Flag Hoisting on Campus) — Holiday after ceremony
• August 19: Parsi New Year (Pateti) — Holiday
• August 28: Raksha Bandhan — Official Holiday

7. SEPTEMBER:
• September 4: Janmashtami (Krishna Janmotsav) — Official Holiday
• September 14: Ganesh Chaturthi / Samvatsari — Official Holiday
• Eid-e-Milad — Official Holiday

8. OCTOBER:
• October 2: Mahatma Gandhi Jayanti — National Holiday
• October 20: Dussehra (Vijayadashami) — Official Holiday
• October 31: Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel Jayanti (National Unity Day) — Official Holiday

9. NOVEMBER (DIWALI VACATION):
• November 7 to November 15: Complete University Diwali Vacation Break (Includes Dhanteras, Kali Chaudas, Diwali, New Year / Nutan Varsh, Bhai Dooj, and Labh Pancham) — University & Institutes Closed
• November 24: Guru Nanak Jayanti — Official Holiday

10. DECEMBER:
• December 25: Christmas — Official Holiday
• Winter Break: Late November to Mid December

=== EXAMINATION POLICIES & ATTENDANCE RULES ===
• Minimum Attendance Requirement: 80% attendance in lectures and practical labs is mandatory to appear in University End Semester Examinations.
• Internal Evaluation Scheme: 30% Continuous Evaluation (Mid-sem exams, quizzes, assignments) + 70% University End Semester Exam.
• Central Library During Exam Days: Air-conditioned reading hall open 24/7 with continuous power backup.
