# CHAROTAR UNIVERSITY OF SCIENCE AND TECHNOLOGY (CHARUSAT)
# OFFICIAL CAMPUS CANTEENS, FOOD COURTS, CAFETERIAS & FOOD HUBS DIRECTORY

================================================================================
1. ON-CAMPUS CENTRAL CANTEENS & CAFETERIAS
================================================================================

1.1 Shreeji Central Canteen (Main Campus Canteen)
• Location: Centrally situated on the main campus between CSPIT, DEPSTAR, and Sports Ground.
• Operating Hours: 8:30 AM to 5:30 PM (Monday to Saturday).
• Food Varieties & Offerings:
  - North Indian & Punjabi: Paneer Butter Masala, Dal Fry, Jeera Rice, Rotis, Punjabi Thalis.
  - Chinese: Veg Manchurian (Dry/Gravy), Hakka Noodles, Schezwan Fried Rice, Spring Rolls.
  - South Indian: Masala Dosa, Mysore Masala Dosa, Plain Dosa, Idli-Sambar, Medu Vada.
  - Fast Food & Snacks: Veg Cheese Grilled Sandwiches, Puffs (Aloo, Cheese, Paneer), Samosas, Batata Vada, French Fries.
  - Beverages: Special Masala Tea, Filter Coffee, Cold Coffee, Fresh Juices, Soft Drinks, and Amul Ice Creams.
• Seating & Hygiene: Spacious indoor and semi-open ventilated seating capacity of 500+ students, purified RO drinking water stations, digital UPI/cash payment enabled.

1.2 Nescafe Coffee Kiosks & Campus Cafeteria
• Located near primary academic plazas (CSPIT/DEPSTAR/CMPICA).
• Menu: Hot Cappuccino, Espresso, Iced Frappes, Cold Coffee, Maggi Noodles (Plain/Cheese), Cookies, Muffins, and Quick Grab-and-Go snacks.

1.3 Amul Dairy & Ice Cream Parlour
• On-campus official booth for Amul Ice Creams, Amul Kool, Masti Dahi, Buttermilk (Chhas), Chocolates, and Cheese Snacks.

1.4 Hostel Dining Messes
• Dedicated dining halls for on-campus Boys and Girls Hostels.
• Serves 4 times daily: Morning Breakfast with Tea/Milk, Afternoon Lunch, Evening Snacks/High Tea, and Night Dinner.
• 100% Pure Vegetarian, hygienic, balanced student nutrition with special sweet/feast meals on Sundays and festivals.

2. POPULAR STUDENT FOOD HUBS & EATERIES (IMMEDIATELY OUTSIDE GATES / CHANGA)
================================================================================

2.1 Lotus Complex & Ramdev Food Hub (Opposite CHARUSAT Hospital & MTIN Gate)
• Tea Post (The Desi Cafe): Premium chai (Ginger, Elaichi, Lemon), Bun Maska, Handvo, Thepla, Mexican Sandwiches, Garlic Toast.
• Hot N Spicy: Highly famous among CHARUSAT students for variety of hot fresh Puffs (Aloo, Cheese Burst, Manchurian, Paneer, Schezwan Puff), Frankies, and Club Sandwiches.
• Kingsman Eatery: Fast food, Burgers, Wraps, Momos, and Mocktails.

2.2 Valetva Road & Hostel Zone Eateries
• Cafe Robusta: Located on Valetva Road opposite SBI Bank, known for late-night coffee, Thick Shakes, Cold Brew, and Cheesy Fries.
• The Hideout Cafe (Near Om Hostel): Popular student lounge for Wood-fired Pizzas, Burgers, Pasta, and Nachos.
• Maggi & Frankie Points: Multiple food stalls specializing in Tadka Maggi, Double Cheese Maggi, Peri Peri Fries, Veg/Paneer/Cheese Frankies, Pav Bhaji, and live Dosa counters.
